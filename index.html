ex out cōgitā think

From ex, out, and cogitare, to think.

eks-KOH-gi-tah Latin verb, imperative of excōgitāre

To think a thing through until it exists.

  1. 1

    Think it out

    An idea usually arrives as a solution. First I work out the problem behind it and who actually has it.

  2. 2

    Plan it

    Decide what gets built first, what waits and what never gets built. The architecture follows from that.

  3. 3

    Bring it into existence

    Write it, ship it and stay for what comes after launch: the bugs, the monitoring, the second version.
